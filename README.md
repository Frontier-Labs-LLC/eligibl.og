# Eligibl

A check a person can finish before they take a number at a county office. Federal and North Carolina aid, plus a map of local doors. The map also lists North Carolina State Parks campgrounds and published safe-parking lots. A screening, not a decision.

North Carolina is the launch state. The catalog is a draft until a state or county office seeds the facts it will stand behind.

Open `#office` for the one-page brief to leave with NCDHHS or a county Department of Social Services.

## Scripts

- `npm start` — local app
- `npm test` — resident entry and the state-office brief
- `npm run build` — production bundle
